Three More From the Editor's Kitchen

The editor is on a roll. Late this morning Kay filed three more recipes in one thread — Genovese Lemon Cookies, Creamy Tuscan Chicken for Two, and her Perfect Oatmeal — each with a note in her own words. All three run below, cards and notes exactly as she sent them.

The Publisher's DeskTuesday, November 17, 2026

This morning's No. 2 carried Kay's first filings: her Almond Biscotti card in Recipes and the Victorian Lounging Hat sewing guide in Personal Projects — both still on this page, below the new arrivals. Before noon, three more emails landed, this time with a few words from the editor herself.

There is a lemon cookie she has been toying with, built on the super tart Genovese lemons she grows. There is her favourite dinner for two, a creamy Tuscan chicken she makes several times a month. And there is her take on oatmeal, which she calls a comfort on a rainy night. Her notes run with each recipe, in her words.

Kay holds full editorial control over this paper. Nothing appears here that she hasn't sent or approved, and she can change anything in it — including who else may contribute — with a single email. Tap any card to read it full-screen.

Recipes

From Kay's kitchen

Kay's Genovese Lemon Cookies

by Kay Korbel

Filed Tuesday, November 17, 2026 · Makes 30 cookies

From Kay's email: “Recently, I've been toying with a lemon cookie recipe. The version below is what I came up with by substituting preserved lemon with fresh Genovese lemon zest and juice. I grow these super tart 🍋 Genovese lemons.” The card carries the full recipe — 20 grams of zest (about 4 lemons), a chill of at least two hours, and a double-sugar roll before baking.

Creamy Tuscan Chicken for Two

by Kay Korbel

Filed Tuesday, November 17, 2026 · For two

From Kay's email: “OH, here is my fav recipe for 2 for Creamy Tuscan Chicken w/ pasta. This could be served with rice, or mashed potatoes… enjoy. I make this several times a month.” Whole wheat fusilli, sun-dried tomatoes, spinach, cream and Reggiano Parmesan — six steps, on the card.

Perfect Oatmeal

by Kay Korbel

Filed Tuesday, November 17, 2026 · Adapted from a Cook's Illustrated recipe · Serves 3 to 4

From Kay's email: “Here is my take on oatmeal, which is such a comfort on a rainy night.” The oats are toasted in butter until they smell of butterscotch, simmered with golden raisins, and served over brown sugar in the bottom of the bowl.

Almond Biscotti

by Kay Korbel

Filed Tuesday, November 17, 2026 · Adapted from America's Test Kitchen · Makes 2 loaves

Kay's first recipe for her Gazette arrives as a finished card: toasted almonds, nutmeg and cinnamon, and seven steps from parchment to a biscotti that keeps a month in an airtight container. The full card is the recipe — every ingredient and instruction is on it.
