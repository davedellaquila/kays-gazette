Kay's Gazette

A short guide for Kay — editor-in-chief

Kay, this paper is yours. It lives at davedellaquila.github.io/kays-gazette, and it exists for your recipes, your personal projects, and anything else you'd like to put in it. You have full editorial control over the content — what runs, where it runs, and how long it stays.

How to publish something

Just send an email to Dave (dave@dellaquila.com) from your own email address, with "Kay's Gazette" in the subject line or the message. Write the item the way you'd like it to read, say which section it belongs in, and attach any photos you'd like to run with it. It will be posted in your section, in your words.

Running the paper

  • Start a new section any time. If recipes and projects aren't enough — a garden column, a travel log, anything — just name the new section in your email and it becomes part of the paper.
  • Feature something. Say "feature this" and that item gets the prominent spot.
  • Change anything. Wording, layout of your sections, who else may contribute — one email from you changes it. For now you are the only contributor; that's yours to change whenever you like.

Two decisions that are yours to make

1. How long should items stay on the front page?

Nothing needs to stay up forever. As a starting point, other local papers retire items after about four days — but this is your call: a few days, a few weeks, or "everything stays until I say take it down." Tell Dave what you'd like and that becomes the paper's rule.

2. Should recipes follow the same rule?

Recipes are the kind of thing people come back for. They can retire on the same schedule as everything else, stay up longer, or stay up permanently — your choice.

That's all there is to it. When you're ready, send your first item — a recipe is a fine way to open a paper.
